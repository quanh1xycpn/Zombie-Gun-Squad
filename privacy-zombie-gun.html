Privacy Policy — Zombie Gun Squad

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zombie Gun Squad ("the App", "we") handles information when you use the App on your mobile device.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There is no account registration, no login, and no submission of personal data. Game progress (such as your current level) is stored only on your device.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your device: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (or reset/delete your Advertising ID).

3. Permissions

The App only requires network access, which is used solely for displaying advertisements. The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services in the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Data Security

Since the App does not collect personal data itself, there is no personal data under our control to secure. Data processed by advertising partners is protected under their respective security policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Bạn có thể tắt quảng cáo cá nhân hóa: Cài đặt → Google → Quảng cáo → Tắt cá nhân hóa quảng cáo.
